# 🌱 GreenPoints – Smart Waste Management System

## 📌 Overview

**GreenPoints** is a smart waste management web application designed to encourage households to properly **segregate waste** and participate in maintaining a clean and healthy community.

The system uses **QR-based household identification** and a **digital reward points system** to motivate people to separate waste into categories such as **wet waste and dry waste**.

Households earn **Green Points** when they properly segregate their waste. These points can be used to provide rewards and recognition for active participation.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Improper waste disposal and lack of waste segregation are common problems in many communities.

People often mix wet and dry waste because of:

* Lack of awareness
* Lack of motivation
* Improper waste collection practices
* No system to track household participation
* Lack of incentives for proper waste segregation

This makes recycling and waste management more difficult and can lead to an unhealthy environment.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

GreenPoints provides a digital platform that connects **households, sanitation workers, and local authorities**.

Each household gets a **unique QR code** linked to its digital profile.

During waste collection:

1. The sanitation worker scans the household's QR code.
2. The type of waste submitted is recorded.
3. The system checks whether the waste is properly segregated.
4. Green Points are awarded to the household.
5. Households can view their points and participation history.
6. Authorities can monitor waste segregation through a dashboard.

## ⭐ Key Features

### 🏠 Household Registration

* Register households in the system.
* Create a unique household profile.
* Generate a unique QR code for each household.

### 📱 QR-Based Identification

* Each household receives a unique QR code.
* Sanitation workers can scan the QR code during collection.
* Household waste collection records can be maintained digitally.

### ♻️ Waste Segregation Tracking

The system can record different types of waste, such as:

* 🟢 Wet Waste
* 🔵 Dry Waste
* 🔴 Other/Improperly Segregated Waste

### 🎁 Green Points

Households receive points for proper waste segregation.

Points can encourage residents to participate regularly and maintain good waste-management practices.

Possible rewards include:

* 🌱 Plants
* ♻️ Recycling-related rewards
* 🏆 Certificates
* ⭐ Community recognition

### 📊 Admin Dashboard

Authorities can view:

* Number of registered households
* Waste collection records
* Waste segregation performance
* Points earned by households
* Participating households
* Community-level statistics

### 📚 Awareness Section

The platform can provide information about:

* Proper waste segregation
* Recycling
* Composting
* Environmental cleanliness
* Importance of reducing waste
